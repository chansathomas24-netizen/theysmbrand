The Occasion

What Is Burgundy in Bloom

The inaugural edition of The YSM Brand Experience — a quarterly gathering, one edition per season, designed to be savoured rather than repeated.

More Than Fine Dining

Burgundy in Bloom is more than a meal — it's an extension of The YSM Brand, built for those who appreciate refined hospitality, thoughtful dining, and meaningful experiences. As nature blooms, so does a new chapter for YSM.

The YSM Brand Experience takes place only four times a year. Each edition carries its own carefully curated theme, with limited guest access to preserve the intimacy, exclusivity, and personalised hospitality that define it. Every gathering is unique — every season tells a different story.

  • A beautifully curated welcome package, delivered ahead of the evening
  • Five thoughtfully crafted courses, plated tableside
  • Carefully selected wine pairings and refreshments throughout
  • Interactive entertainment and genuine connection among guests
  • A meaningful farewell gift to remember the evening by
